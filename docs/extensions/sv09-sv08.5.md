# sv09 and sv08.5: Journey Together, Prismatic Evolutions (PLAN-B B11)

sv09 157/157. sv08.5: every Standard definition implemented (133 of 146 counting the non-Standard cards it reprints,
outside PLAN-B's scope). Hand scripts: `packages/cards/src/scripts/sv09.ts` (with the earlier Standard cards reprinted
in both sets). Tests: `packages/cards/test/sv09-sv08.5.test.ts`.

## Generic additions (rules 14)

- Engine:
  - Knock Out events carry the attacker (`ev:attacker`: Maractus, Gengar ex's Fainting Spell);
  - `onAttach.heal` (Magearna), `evolvesAs` (Eevee ex's Rainbow DNA), `devolve.to` "deck" (Espeon ex);
  - the `winGame` op and the end reason "effect" (N's Sigilyph; the client shows it);
  - `attackCost.attack` and `.anyType` (Bloodmoon Ursaluna ex, Sparkling Crystal);
  - `ignoreEffects` (Walking Wake ex);
  - `AbilityDef.onlyByEffect` (Palafin ex);
  - Pokémon filter `counters` (exact damage counters).
- Static grammar: "your {G} Pokémon and {R} Pokémon", "whenever you attach an Energy card from your hand to 1 of your
  Pokémon, heal N damage from that Pokémon", "ignore all Energy in the cost of <attack>"; "The attacks it uses…"
  after a condition.
- Templates:
  - coins: "If at least N of them are heads", "If heads, this attack does N more damage, and …" (combined templates come
    first: a shorter template would take their first sentence);
  - "You may discard your hand. If you discarded any cards…", "<Pokémon> is Knocked Out.", "Then, draw a card for each
    …", "Then, if …, draw N more cards.", "Discard a card from your hand. If you do, draw N cards.";
  - Tools discarded before damage with a Paralysis rider, "You can use this attack only if this Pokémon used <attack>
    during your last turn" (Miltank);
  - KO triggers "If this Pokémon is in the Active Spot and is Knocked Out…", "switch it with your Active Pokémon";
  - phrases: "Beldum and Metang are on your Bench", "Energy attached to all of your Iono's Pokémon", "Stage 2 Pokémon
    on your Bench", Special Energy by name ("Spiky Energy cards").
