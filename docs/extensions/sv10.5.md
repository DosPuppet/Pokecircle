sv10.5b 86/86 (72 before the lot), sv10.5w 87/87 (64 before). Hand scripts: `packages/cards/src/scripts/sv10.5.ts`.
Tests: `packages/cards/test/sv10.5.test.ts`.

## Generic additions (rules 12)

- Engine:
  - `evolveFromHand.fromDeck` and `.target` (evolution from the deck: Duosion, Reuniclus, Vivillon);
  - the `firstTurnAttacks` rule (Meloetta ex's Debut Performance);
  - the `attackBase` rule ("During your next turn, this Pokémon's Hyper Fang attack's base damage is 240");
  - Pokémon filter `resistance` (Boldore).
- Phrases: "you have N or fewer Benched Pokémon", "<Pokémon> is on your Bench", "has {F} Resistance", "isn't a Pokémon
  ex", "Energy attached to it", "before this attack does damage" (dropped: checks come first anyway), "If either of them
  is heads" (= any of them).
- Static grammar: "Prevent all damage from and effects of attacks done to this Pokémon by your opponent's Pokémon that
  have any Special Energy attached", two kinds of cards locked at once (Jellicent ex), "If you go first, this Pokémon
  can use attacks during your first turn".
- Templates: healing "it" and each Pokémon of a kind, optional revealed searches, Energy moves (opponent's Pokémon,
  Bench to Active, a Basic Energy to the Bench), attaching a Basic Energy from the hand to this Pokémon, the opponent
  shuffling their hand and drawing, a Benched Pokémon back to the hand, moving damage counters to an opponent's Pokémon,
  devolving an opponent's Pokémon, one Basic Pokémon from the discard pile, damage per card in a revealed hand,
  discarding all Energy of a type for damage, Ability hand costs with any card description.
