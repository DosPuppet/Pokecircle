# me05 (PLAN-B B5)

88 of 88 definitions implemented (52 before the lot). 28 came from templates and phrases made generic for every set,
8 from hand scripts (`packages/cards/src/scripts/me05.ts`). Tests: `packages/cards/test/me05.test.ts`.

## Generic additions (rules 8)

- Phrases: "you have no cards in your hand", "exactly N Prize cards remaining", "N or more damage counters on it",
  a card attached by name ("Voltaic {L} Energy" → Voltaic Lightning Energy, `attachedNamed`), "for each of your
  Benched Pokémon that has any {R} Energy attached" and other filtered counts, Pokémon filters "that has any
  ({T} / Special) Energy attached", "with N or less Energy attached", "Burned Pokémon" (`status`), "Mega Evolution
  Pokémon ex", "that has "Antique" in its name", Pokémon searched by name ("up to 3 Lampent").
- Pokémon named by a search go onto the Bench whatever their Stage (`move.anyStage`: Charjabug, Lampent, Silcoon).
- "During your (opponent's) next turn, <a static sentence>": the static grammar as a timed effect (taken more or less
  damage, prevention from a kind of Pokémon, more damage dealt, damage counters on the Attacking Pokémon).
- `retaliate` (damage counters on the Attacking Pokémon when damaged, even if Knocked Out); `survive` with a coin flip
  or without the full HP requirement (Annihilape).
- Triggers read the facts of their event (`ev:<key>` variables): `wasActive`, the damage done (`amount`), the cards
  attached to a Knocked Out Pokémon (`attached`). "If this Pokémon is in the Active Spot and is damaged…" now holds
  even if it was Knocked Out by that damage.
- Delayed effects: `endOfTurn` (a timed rule whose effects resolve at the end of its last turn on each Pokémon it is
  bound to); it ends with the other effects of attacks when the Pokémon leaves the Active Spot (R-TEXT-4).
- Coins of an attack flipped again once per turn (`reflip`, Backtrack Badge): a yes/no question after the flips.
- `chooseCards.bottom` (the bottom N cards of the deck); card filter `attackNamed`.
- "Place N damage counters" is read as "Put"; "Look at the top N cards of your deck, and you may …" as two sentences;
  "(This includes new Pokémon that come into play.)" is the default of an effect on a scope.
