# sv06: Twilight Masquerade (PLAN-B B14)

Every Standard definition with a sv06 print implemented (168 of 170: Raifort and Luminous Energy are non-Standard
reprints, out of scope). Hand scripts: `packages/cards/src/scripts/sv06.ts`. Tests: `packages/cards/test/sv06.test.ts`.

## Generic additions (rules 17)

- `noToHand`: the scope's Pokémon and their attached cards can't be put into the hand (Milotic's Mentally Calm); checked
  by `pokemonTo`, `devolve`, Energy sent to the hand and `move` of cards in play (`keptFromHand`, `statics.ts`).
- Card filter `nameInPlay`: the same name as a Pokémon of a side in play (Love Ball).
- `replaceCard.old`: the replaced card may go to the deck (Palafin's Zero to Hero switches in Palafin ex from the deck).
- A played Trainer's resolution stores the card as `thisCard` (Caretaker shuffles itself into the deck).
- A stored card in play stands for its Pokémon in a Pokémon reference, and `moveEnergy` may target a reference (Jynx
  moves an Energy to "the new Benched Pokémon").
