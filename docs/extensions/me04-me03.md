# me04 and me03 (PLAN-B B6)

me04 90/90 (50 before the lot), me03 92/92 (66 before). Hand scripts: `packages/cards/src/scripts/me04.ts`,
`packages/cards/src/scripts/me03.ts`. Tests: `packages/cards/test/me04-me03.test.ts`.

## Generic additions (rules 9)

- Phrases: "you have the same number of cards in your hand as your opponent", "you played <card> from your hand during
  this turn", "<card> is in your discard pile", "has no Retreat Cost", "your opponent has exactly N cards in their
  hand", "you have <Pokémon> in play"; counts "of your Beedrill and Beedrill ex in play", "of your Benched Pokémon that
  has any damage counters on it"; scopes by name ("your Benched Rattata"); Trainer cards by name ("up to 2
  Transformation Tome cards"); "Ancient card" / "Future card" are tags, not names.
- Templates: Basic Pokémon from the discard pile onto the Bench, moving all damage counters to the opponent's Active
  Pokémon, healing every Pokémon, Knocking Out low-HP Pokémon, Energy from the Bench to the Active Pokémon, attaching
  from the hand to a kind of Pokémon, discard-from-hand damage (with or without "may"), Ability costs "discard a … from
  your hand in order to use this Ability", stronger Poison, "Choose 1 of your opponent's Active Pokémon's attacks …",
  attack flips with 2 coins, "Energy can't be attached … to the Defending Pokémon", "their Poisoned Pokémon can't
  retreat".
- Static grammar: "do N less damage" (attacker filters such as "that has a Pokémon Tool attached"), "ignore all {C}
  Energy in the costs", "can evolve during your first turn or the turn you play it" (`evolveSameTurn.firstTurn`),
  Ability-effect immunity (`shielded`), coin-flip prevention (`prevent.flip`, flipped when the damage is done),
  `noMoveCounters`, `retreatFlip`, a trailing "doesn't stack" on any text.
- Engine: `cantAttach`, `attackFlip.coins`, the `weakTo` condition, `TrainerDef.replaces` (a Stadium put into play by
  discarding another, the same turn), `replaceCard` (a card takes the place of a Pokémon's top card), Energy returned
  to the hand after the attack (`returnAfterAttack: "hand"`, `returnFilter`), the `discardedFromDeck` trigger (cards
  milled by the opponent), Knock Out events carry the Pokémon's cards, `attach.chooser`, `gatherEnergy.types`, card
  filter `resistance`.
