# sv08: Surging Sparks (PLAN-B B12)

Every Standard definition with a sv08 print implemented (192 of 193; Jet Energy, a non-Standard reprint, is out of
scope). Hand scripts: `packages/cards/src/scripts/sv08.ts`. Tests: `packages/cards/test/sv08.test.ts`.

## Generic additions (rules 15)

- The duration `untilYourNextTurnEnd` ("Until the end of your next turn": Oranguru).
- An attack clause may hold two effects joined by ", and" ("is now Paralyzed, and discard an Energy from that
  Pokémon").
- Phrases: "there are N or fewer cards in your deck", "your opponent has N or more Benched Pokémon", "your opponent has no
  Pokémon ex … in play", "you don't have Uxie and Azelf on your Bench".
- Templates: "If your opponent's Active Pokémon is a {N} Pokémon, it is now Paralyzed", "Move all Energy from this
  Pokémon to 1 of your Benched Pokémon", "You may draw N cards", "Heal N damage from your Active {N} Pokémon", "Discard
  a Stadium in play. If you can't, this attack does nothing"; static "… can't attack" under a condition (Slaking ex).
