# me02.5 (PLAN-B B7)

225 of 225 definitions with a me02.5 print implemented (the set reprints many earlier cards). Hand scripts:
`packages/cards/src/scripts/me02.5.ts`. Tests: `packages/cards/test/me02.5.test.ts`.

## Generic additions (rules 10)

- Turn log: the attack used (`attacked` with the attack's name) and Prize cards taken (`prizesTaken`, `amount`); the
  condition `didLastTurn` ("If this Pokémon used Spiky Rolling during your last turn": Togedemaru ex, Falinks,
  Weezing); amount `turnLog.sum` ("for each Prize card your opponent took during their last turn").
- `dealt.attack`: a bonus for one named attack ("During your next turn, this Pokémon's Mochi Rush attack does 50 more
  damage": Pecharunt, Metagross, Lucario ex); `dealt` "for each …" amounts in the static grammar (Kingambit).
- Trigger `oppToBench` ("Whenever your opponent's Active Pokémon moves to the Bench during their turn": Team Rocket's
  Dugtrio, Magcargo).
- Prize rules: `flip` (Togekiss' Wonder Kiss), `by` (only when the Knocking Out attacker matches: Mega Gengar ex, Anthea &
  Concordia).
- `noTools` (Jamming Tower): Tools give no rules, attacks or triggers while it is in play.
- Phrases: "you have at least N {T} Energy in play", "you (don't) have N or more … in your discard pile", "any of your
  <Pokémon> were Knocked Out … during your opponent's last turn", "is Burned or Poisoned", "you played <card> from
  your hand (during) this turn" with any card description, type lists ("{G}, {R}, {W}, or {L} Pokémon"), named
  holders ("the Pikachu ex this card is attached to").
- Templates: self-discard for damage ("Discard up to 5 {R} Energy from this Pokémon. This attack does 70 damage for
  each card…", with a number choice), damage per card in the revealed hand, "does N damage to each of 2 of …", Energy
  moves to and from this Pokémon, "Each player draws N cards", "Then, discard that Stadium.", optional searches, a
  filtered switch, Surfer, Banette, a Prize-count range, Ability costs paid with this Pokémon's Energy.
