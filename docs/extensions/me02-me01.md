# me02 and me01 (PLAN-B B8)

me02 98/98, me01 135/135 (definitions with a print in the set). Hand scripts: `packages/cards/src/scripts/me02.ts`,
`packages/cards/src/scripts/me01.ts`. Tests: `packages/cards/test/me02-me01.test.ts`.

## Generic additions (rules 11)

- Engine:
  - `AttackDef.altCost` ("If this Pokémon has any damage counters on it, this attack can be used for {D}": Crawdaunt,
    Grapploct);
  - triggers `movedToBench` (this Pokémon from the Active Spot to the Bench: Clawitzer) and `allyBecameActive`
    (another of your Pokémon becomes Active: Latios);
  - `onAttach` (damage counters or the end of the turn when Energy is attached from the hand: Pachirisu);
  - `keepConfused` (Dizzying Valley);
  - `choosePokemon.leastHp` (Inteleon);
  - `takePrizes.side` (Lt. Surge's Bargain);
  - `useCardEffects` (a discarded Supporter's effect: Ninetales);
  - Stadium conditions by owner ("If you have a Stadium in play");
  - the `myTurn` condition;
  - `AbilityDef.setupActive` (Cinderace may be the Active Pokémon at setup; the client places it only in the Active
    Spot).
- Phrases: "{R} Mega Evolution Pokémon ex", "{R} or {W} Pokémon", ", and if …" conditions, "evolved from … during
  this turn", "for each Pokémon Tool attached to all Pokémon", "Pokémon that has "Tauros" in its name that has any
  damage counters on it".
- Templates:
  - "Discard N … from your hand. If you can't…, this attack does nothing.";
  - mill-for-damage ("Discard the top 6 cards of your deck, and this attack does 100 damage for each … you
    discarded");
  - "You may <discard / shuffle / put into your hand> all Energy … and have this attack do N more damage";
  - "this attack's base damage is N";
  - "Discard any amount of {R} Energy from among your Pokémon";
  - Energy moves and attachments from the hand to this Pokémon or the deck top;
  - random hand cards shuffled back, revealing the hand and discarding;
  - "Make your opponent's Active Pokémon Confused";
  - "As often as you like during your turn, if …" Abilities.
- Fixed: "Switch this Pokémon with your Active Pokémon" now switches this Pokémon (it let the player choose any
  Benched Pokémon).
