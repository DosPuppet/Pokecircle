## Generic additions (rules 13)

- Engine:
  - triggers `allyKnockedOut` (event `pokemon`, `defId`, `attached`, `byAttack`) and `oppEvolved`;
  - `TriggerDef.noStack` (one queued trigger per player, key and event: "The effect of Darkest Impulse doesn't
    stack");
  - the condition `eventCard` (the card of the event matches a filter);
  - the rule `cantPlayPokemon` (Team Rocket's Arbok), card filter `hasAbility`;
  - `retaliate.asDamage` (Zamazenta);
  - `discardEnergy.names`, `pokemonTo` "deckTop", the card destination "prizes".
- Templates:
  - delayed effects at the end of the opponent's next turn: discard, damage counters, Knock Out (Jynx, Team Rocket's
    Grimer, Glaceon, Pinsir);
  - "Move N Energy from this Pokémon to 1 of your Benched Pokémon", drawing from the bottom of the deck, ordering the
    top of the opponent's deck, looking at an opponent's Prize card;
  - "Choose N of your opponent's Pokémon and put N damage counters on each of them", optional switch of the opponent's
    Active Pokémon, "If …, draw N more cards";
  - discard-and-Knock-Out costs, optional Stadium discard for damage, Bench damage to damaged Pokémon, damage per
    counter on a Benched Pokémon, "the next turn, counters equal to the damage done".
- Ability forms: "During Pokémon Checkup, if this Pokémon is in the Active Spot, …", "when this Pokémon moves from your
  Bench to the Active Spot, you may …"; statics "prevent all effects of Item or Supporter cards done to this Pokémon"
  and the Pokémon lock.
