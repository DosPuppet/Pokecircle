# sv07 + sv06.5: Stellar Crown, Shrouded Fable (PLAN-B B13)

Every Standard definition with a sv07 or sv06.5 print implemented (sv07 144 of 145: Blastoise ex is a non-Standard
reprint, out of scope; sv06.5 70 of 70). Hand scripts: `packages/cards/src/scripts/sv07.ts`. Tests:
`packages/cards/test/sv07-sv06.5.test.ts`.

## Generic additions (rules 16)

- The `shielded` rule ("can't be affected by your opponent's Abilities / Trainers") is enforced in
  `attackEffectsPrevented`; it gains the `supporters` source (Rhyperior's Wide Wall).
- Confusion with more damage counters: `condition.confusion` and the `confusion` field of a Pokémon's conditions (Lanturn).
- `AbilityDef.benchFromHand`: a non-Basic Pokémon put onto the Bench from the hand while a condition holds (Klinklang).
- `evolveFromHand.store`: the evolved Pokémon is stored, to evolve it again (Grand Tree).
- ACE SPEC "This card can't be put into your hand or deck from the discard pile" compiles to `TrainerDef.noRecovery`:
  the card is never offered by discard-pile choices.
- Templates and phrases: "You can use this card only when it is the last card in your hand"; name pairs as scopes
  ("your Drifloon and Drifblim"). Drifblim, Cassiopeia, Neutralization Zone and Poké Vital A compile.

- Cresselia's Crescent Purge: the Prize card is picked at random and revealed to both players; it does not stay face up
  (Prize cards are hidden from both players).
